# Module 0.3 — What Should Be Automated?

## Goal
Avoid automating tasks merely because automation is technically possible.

## Strong candidates
Tasks are often good automation candidates when they are:
- frequent
- rule-driven
- repetitive
- expensive in human attention
- cross-system but well-defined
- measurable
- tolerant of controlled automation

## Warning signs
Be careful when:
- the process changes every week
- inputs are poorly defined
- mistakes have severe consequences
- ownership is unclear
- the task requires nuanced judgment with no review path
- the process should be eliminated rather than automated

## Exercise
Rank five hypothetical processes from "automate now" to "do not automate" and justify the ranking using risk, frequency, clarity, and value.
