# Module 0.2 — Process Discovery

## Goal
Turn vague complaints into a process you can reason about.

## Questions to ask
- What starts the process?
- Who performs each step now?
- What information is required?
- Where does that information come from?
- What decisions are made?
- Which decisions are deterministic and which require judgment?
- What happens when information is missing?
- What systems are touched?
- What must never happen twice?
- What needs approval?
- What is the cost of failure?
- How do we know the process succeeded?

## Exercise
Complaint: "Our sales team keeps losing leads and entering the same customer twice. We also forget to follow up."

Write:
1. at least ten discovery questions
2. a current-state process
3. likely failure points
4. what you would refuse to automate until clarified

Do not design a solution yet.
