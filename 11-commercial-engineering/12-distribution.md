# Module 11.12 — Distribution & Outreach

## Capability
Create repeatable ways for the right people to encounter proof.

## Core model
Channels can include targeted direct outreach, social/content, communities, agencies, partnerships, job boards/contracts and marketplace profiles. Measure response.

## Practice
Run small ethical outreach/application experiment after gate.

## Failure / transfer task
Compare generic mass pitch vs problem-specific proof.

## Evidence to save
Tracker with response/learning.

## Mastery
You adapt channel/message from evidence.
