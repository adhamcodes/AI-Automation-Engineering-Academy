# Phase 11 — Commercial Engineering

**Typical effort:** Runs alongside later technical phases

## Purpose
Learn the commercial engineering needed to discover, scope, demonstrate, deliver and support automation work without promising nonsense.

## Modules
1. Problem Discovery
2. Choose a Problem Category / Market
3. Discovery Conversations
4. Scope & Acceptance
5. Demos & Proof of Capability
6. Pricing Models
7. Platform Licensing & Ownership
8. Client Security & Data Responsibility
9. Documentation & Handover
10. Maintenance & Support Boundaries
11. Case Studies & Portfolio
12. Distribution & Outreach
13. Commercial Delivery Gate

## Gate
Pass **Commercial Delivery Ready** before taking ownership of serious paid systems independently.
