# Module 11.2 — Choose a Problem Category / Market

## Capability
Narrow initial commercial learning enough to build reusable insight.

## Core model
Focus can be by workflow/problem (lead intake, onboarding, reporting) rather than permanent industry niche.

## Practice
Score categories by pain, access, repetition, integration feasibility and risk.

## Failure / transfer task
Avoid category requiring regulated/high-liability expertise you do not have.

## Evidence to save
90-day focus hypothesis.

## Mastery
Focus is testable and reversible.
