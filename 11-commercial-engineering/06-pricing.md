# Module 11.6 — Pricing Models

## Capability
Price scope/risk/value/support consciously rather than copying internet numbers.

## Core model
Fixed, milestone, hourly/retainer, support and productized pricing fit different uncertainty. Include infrastructure/API costs and maintenance ownership.

## Practice
Price three hypothetical scopes with assumptions.

## Failure / transfer task
Identify underpriced project with high unknown API/data risk.

## Evidence to save
Pricing worksheet.

## Mastery
You can explain what changes price.
