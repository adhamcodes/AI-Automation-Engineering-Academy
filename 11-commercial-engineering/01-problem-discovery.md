# Module 11.1 — Problem Discovery

## Capability
Find costly repetitive process problems instead of selling tools.

## Core model
Look for frequency, labor, delay, errors, missed revenue/response, coordination friction and measurable outcome.

## Practice
Analyze 20 businesses/processes from public observations or interviews.

## Failure / transfer task
Reject “AI chatbot” where no clear process/value exists.

## Evidence to save
Problem inventory with evidence.

## Mastery
You can describe pain without mentioning n8n/AI.
