# Weekly Execution Model — 2–3 hours/day

A simple default, not a prison:

- **3 sessions:** curriculum + focused exercises.
- **2 sessions:** build/integration work.
- **1 session:** failure injection, debugging, documentation, and portfolio proof.
- **1 session:** commercial discovery once Gate 3/4 approaches: study real processes, improve a demo, publish proof, apply, or talk to potential users.

Do not consume seven tutorials in a week. Finish with a system, test evidence, or a concrete capability.
