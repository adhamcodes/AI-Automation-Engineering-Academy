# Module 09.4 — Approvals & Decision Rights

## Capability
Require humans for consequential actions.

## Core model
Preview proposed action, evidence and uncertainty; record approve/edit/reject and resume idempotently.

## Practice
Approval before customer message/account change.

## Failure / transfer task
Handle duplicate approval and timeout.

## Evidence to save
Approval workflow.

## Mastery
No approval means no consequential side effect.
