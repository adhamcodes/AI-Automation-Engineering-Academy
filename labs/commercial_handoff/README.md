# Commercial Handoff Lab

Take one completed automation and produce a client handoff pack containing:

- scope and exclusions,
- architecture diagram,
- credentials/ownership inventory without exposing secrets,
- setup and deployment steps,
- monitoring and alert behavior,
- failure/recovery runbook,
- support boundary,
- change-request process,
- acceptance evidence.

Pass when another engineer could operate the system without depending on undocumented knowledge in your head.
