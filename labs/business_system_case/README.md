# Business System Case

A small company receives support requests by email, website form, and chat. Requests can duplicate, contain missing data, or require approval before refunds.

Produce:

- current-state map,
- failure/risk map,
- target architecture,
- source-of-truth decision,
- idempotency strategy,
- human approval boundary,
- observability plan,
- acceptance tests,
- rollback plan,
- measurable business outcome.

Do not choose tools until the process and failure model are explicit.
