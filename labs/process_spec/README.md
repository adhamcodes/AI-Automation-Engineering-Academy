# Process Specification Lab

Take a messy process such as "new customer request arrives by email and must become a tracked task." Produce:

- trigger,
- inputs,
- decisions,
- actions,
- outputs,
- state,
- exceptions,
- human handoffs,
- failure consequences.

Then identify what must remain deterministic, what may use AI, and where human approval is required.

Pass when another person could implement the process from your specification without guessing critical behavior.
