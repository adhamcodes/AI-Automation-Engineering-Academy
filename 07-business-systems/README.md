# Phase 7 — Business Automation Systems

**Typical effort:** ~3–4 weeks

## Purpose
Practice end-to-end operational automation categories and, more importantly, requirements discovery/scoping from messy real-world language.

## Modules
1. CRM & Lead Operations
2. Customer/Employee Onboarding
3. Customer Support Operations
4. Approval Workflows
5. Reporting & Scheduled Operations
6. Cross-System Data Synchronization
7. Admin & Document Workflows
8. Requirements Discovery
9. Scoping & Acceptance Criteria
10. Business Systems Capstone

## Gate
Pass **Client-Grade Automation Builder** by converting an ambiguous process into an explicit, testable, maintainable system.
