# Module 07.1 — CRM & Lead Operations

## Capability
Map lead capture/enrichment/dedup/routing/follow-up into reliable stateful automation.

## Core model
Business identity, consent/source, ownership, duplicate prevention and status transitions matter more than the CRM brand.

## Practice
Design lead flow from form to owner and follow-up.

## Failure / debugging task
Handle duplicate lead, missing owner and API outage.

## Evidence to save
CRM process map + working simulation.

## Mastery
No lead disappears or triggers duplicate outreach.
