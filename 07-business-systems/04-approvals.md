# Module 07.4 — Approval Workflows

## Capability
Design explicit decision rights and audit trails.

## Core model
Approval requires request context, approver identity, deadline, decision capture and idempotent continuation.

## Practice
Expense/content/access-style approval.

## Failure / debugging task
Duplicate approval/rejection or approver unavailable.

## Evidence to save
Approval workflow.

## Mastery
No side effect occurs without valid decision.
