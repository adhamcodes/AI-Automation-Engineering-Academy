# Module 06.12 — Cost, Latency & Privacy

## Capability
Operate AI calls within budgets and data constraints.

## Core model
Choose model/context by task, batch/cache where safe, minimize sensitive data, define retention/provider policy.

## Practice
Measure cost/latency per workflow run.

## Failure / debugging task
Detect PII sent unnecessarily or retry storm doubling spend.

## Evidence to save
Budget/privacy checklist.

## Mastery
AI component has explicit operational boundaries.
