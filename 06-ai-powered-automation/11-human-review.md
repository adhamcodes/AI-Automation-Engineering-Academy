# Module 06.11 — Human Review & Exception Queues

## Capability
Design review when confidence/consequence demands it.

## Core model
Send reviewer enough source/context/proposed action; record approve/edit/reject and resume idempotently.

## Practice
Build approval queue for high-value lead or finance-like action.

## Failure / debugging task
Handle no response, rejection and duplicate approval event.

## Evidence to save
Approval-state workflow.

## Mastery
Human decision is durable and auditable.
