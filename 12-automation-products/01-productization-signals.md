# Module 12.1 — Productization Signals

## Capability
Recognize repeated problem/architecture before creating a product.

## Core model
Productization is justified by repeated demand, similar inputs/outputs, stable integration surface and support economics—not desire for passive income.

## Practice
Compare three custom projects and identify reusable core.

## Failure / transfer task
Reject product idea built from one unvalidated workflow.

## Evidence to save
Productization decision memo.

## Mastery
You can name repeated evidence.
