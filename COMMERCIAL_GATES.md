# Commercial Gates

## Gate 1 — Useful Workflow Builder
After core n8n workflow engineering. Build demonstrations; do not yet take ownership of high-risk production systems.

## Gate 2 — Reliable Integration Builder
After persistent state/idempotency. You can build small restart-safe systems.

## Gate 3 — Client-Grade Automation Builder
After business-systems work. You can discover requirements, scope and integrate a realistic process.

## Gate 4 — Monetization Ready
After evaluated AI automation capability. Begin low-risk market tests: applications/contracts, agency implementation help, direct problem-specific outreach, or narrow productized service hypotheses. No income guarantee.

## Gate 5 — Automation Engineer
After production reliability/security. You can own deployed automation with monitoring, backups, incident response and security boundaries.

## Gate 6 — Reusable Product/System Builder
After productization work. You can extract a repeated problem into reusable architecture while understanding isolation, licensing and support burden.
