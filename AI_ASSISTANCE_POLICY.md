# AI Assistance Policy

- Use AI freely for brainstorming, explanation, documentation discovery, test-case ideas, and later professional builds when the task allows it.
- In mastery assessments, follow the stated restriction; many gates require the core implementation/specification without generated answers.
- Never paste client secrets, private data, credentials, or sensitive documents into external AI systems without explicit permission and an appropriate data policy.
- AI-generated workflow/code must be inspected, tested with failure cases, and understood before shipping.

The goal is not “no AI.” The goal is **no dependency disguised as competence**.
